## 4.1 Purpose

ShabaAutos Dealer Membership provides professional vehicle sellers and eligible automotive businesses with additional marketplace features.

## 4.2 Eligibility

Applicants may be required to provide:

- personal identification;
- business name;
- business registration information where applicable;
- address;
- telephone number;
- email;
- supporting vehicle dealership information; and
- other reasonable verification documents.

ShabaAutos may approve or reject membership applications based on its marketplace requirements.

## 4.3 Membership Plans

Different membership plans may provide different:

- listing allowances;
- promotional features;
- dealership profile features;
- enquiry tools;
- analytics;
- priority placement;
- verification services; and
- customer support levels.

The features included in each plan will be displayed before subscription.

## 4.4 Dealer Dashboard

Approved dealer members may receive access to a ShabaAutos dashboard through which they can manage:

- dealership profile;
- vehicle listings;
- customer enquiries;
- membership plan;

- billing;
- payments;
- account information; and
- other available dealer tools.

## 4.5 Dealer Responsibilities

Dealers must ensure that:

- listings are truthful;
- vehicle photographs represent the advertised vehicle;
- vehicle prices are accurate;
- known material defects are disclosed;
- vehicle documentation is legitimate;
- advertised vehicles are legally available for sale;
- sold vehicles are promptly marked as unavailable; and
- customer information is handled responsibly.

## 4.6 Verified Dealer Status

Paid membership does not automatically confer Verified Dealer status.

Verification may require separate checks.

ShabaAutos may withdraw verification where the dealer:

- provides misleading information;
- receives substantiated serious complaints;
- fails verification requirements;
- breaches platform rules; or
- engages in fraudulent or unlawful conduct.

## 4.7 Subscription Payments

Membership charges will be displayed before payment.

Where automatic renewal is available, this will be disclosed to the dealer before activation.

Dealers may cancel future renewal in accordance with the applicable subscription process.

## 4.8 Listing Limits

Each membership tier may have a maximum number of active listings.

Additional listings may require:

- upgrading the plan;
- purchasing additional listing capacity; or
- paying an individual listing fee.

## 4.9 Customer Leads

ShabaAutos does not guarantee a particular number of enquiries, sales or revenue from dealer membership.

Membership provides access to marketplace services and features, not guaranteed commercial results.

## 4.10 Suspension

ShabaAutos may temporarily suspend a dealer account while investigating:

- suspected fraud;
- customer complaints;
- false listings;
- documentation concerns;
- payment issues;
- safety concerns; or
- breaches of these Terms.

## 4.11 Termination

Serious or repeated breaches may result in termination.

Where legally appropriate, dealers will be informed of the reason for termination.

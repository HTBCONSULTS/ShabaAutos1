## 3.1 General Principle

Refund eligibility depends on the service purchased, the stage at which cancellation occurs, costs already incurred and applicable consumer protection law.

Nothing in this policy removes any statutory rights available to a customer.

## 3.2 Vehicle Reservations and Deposits

Where a customer pays a vehicle reservation fee or deposit, the applicable cancellation conditions will be communicated before payment.

A reasonable deduction may apply where ShabaAutos or a seller has incurred costs as a direct result of the reservation, where permitted by law and properly disclosed.

## 3.3 Cancellation by ShabaAutos

Where ShabaAutos cancels a paid service and no equivalent service is provided, the customer will normally receive a refund of amounts paid for the cancelled service, subject to any lawful deductions or circumstances explained to the customer.

## 3.4 Dealer Membership

Dealer subscription fees may become non-refundable once the relevant membership period has started and membership services have been made available, except where:

- ShabaAutos fails to provide the contracted service;
- a refund is required by law; or
- ShabaAutos expressly agrees otherwise.

Cancelling a subscription prevents future renewal where applicable but does not automatically create a refund for time already used.

## 3.5 Vehicle Listings

Once a paid vehicle listing has been published, the listing fee will generally not be refundable merely because the vehicle does not sell.

This does not affect rights arising where ShabaAutos fails to provide the purchased listing service.

## 3.6 Featured Listings and Advertising

Payments for promotional placement already delivered are generally non-refundable.

Future promotional services that have not started may be eligible for cancellation subject to any costs already incurred.

## 3.7 Vehicle Import Services

Import transactions may involve third-party and non-recoverable costs.

Once ShabaAutos has:

- sourced a vehicle;
- purchased a vehicle;
- paid an auction;
- commissioned an inspection;
- arranged transportation;
- booked shipping; or
- incurred another authorised third-party expense,

the corresponding costs may no longer be refundable.

Customers will be informed of material commitments wherever reasonably practicable.

## 3.8 Rental Cancellations

Rental cancellation conditions may depend on:

- notice provided;
- vehicle category;
- booking period;
- third-party costs; and
- whether the rental period has already commenced.

Any specific cancellation fee should be displayed before booking confirmation.

## 3.9 Security Deposits

Rental security deposits are separate from rental charges.

Deposits will be returned subject to:

- vehicle condition;
- outstanding rental charges;
- fuel obligations;
- traffic fines;
- damage;
- late return charges;
- missing equipment; and
- other amounts properly due under the rental agreement.

## 3.10 Processing Refunds

Approved refunds will be returned through an appropriate payment method.

Bank, card or payment provider processing times may affect when the funds become visible to the customer.

These policies govern the use of the ShabaAutos website, marketplace and related services.

ShabaAutos provides services relating to vehicle buying, selling, third-party vehicle listings, rentals, vehicle sourcing and importation, dealer membership and other automotive services.

## Customer Support & Complaints

Questions regarding these policies may be directed to ShabaAutos through the contact channels published on the website.

Customers should provide:

- name;
- contact details;
- relevant booking, listing or transaction reference;
- description of the issue; and
- supporting documents where appropriate.

ShabaAutos will aim to investigate complaints fairly and communicate an appropriate response.

## Policy Changes

ShabaAutos may update these policies as its services, technology, business model or legal obligations change.

The latest version will be published on the ShabaAutos website with the relevant effective or revision date.

Material changes affecting an existing contractual relationship will be communicated where reasonably required.

## Important

These policies should be read together with any specific quotation, purchase agreement, rental agreement, import instruction, membership plan or other transaction-specific document issued by ShabaAutos.

Where applicable law provides customers with rights that cannot legally be excluded or restricted, those rights will continue to apply.

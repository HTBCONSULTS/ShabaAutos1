## 6.1 Principle

ShabaAutos aims to communicate applicable charges before customers commit to a paid transaction.

Browsing vehicles may be free, while selected marketplace and automotive services may attract charges.

## 6.2 Buyers

Ordinary customers will not be required to purchase a paid membership simply to browse available vehicles.

Additional charges may apply for optional services such as:

- vehicle inspection;

- verification;
- delivery;
- sourcing;
- finance-related partner services; or
- other premium services.

## 6.3 Private Sellers

Private sellers may be charged for:

- vehicle listing;
- listing renewal;
- featured placement;
- premium promotion;
- inspection;
- verification; or
- transaction support.

Applicable charges will be displayed before purchase.

## 6.4 Dealers

Dealers may select from available membership plans.

Depending on the plan, fees may cover:

- a specified number of vehicle listings;
- dealership profile;
- dashboard access;
- enquiry management;
- featured placement allowances;
- analytics;
- verification features; and
- other dealer services.

Exact pricing should be published on the Dealer Membership page once approved by ShabaAutos.

## 6.5 Featured Listings

Sellers may be able to purchase additional visibility through:

- Featured Vehicle;

- Priority Listing;
- Homepage Placement;
- Sponsored Listing; or
- other promotional products.

Purchasing promotion does not guarantee a sale.

## 6.6 Sales and Transaction Fees

Where ShabaAutos provides transaction support or acts as an intermediary in a sale, a service or success fee may apply.

The applicable fee will be disclosed before the seller or buyer becomes contractually committed to that service.

## 6.7 Rental Charges

Rental charges may include:

- base rental price;
- security deposit;
- delivery or collection;
- additional driver;
- excess mileage;
- fuel charges;
- late return charges;
- damage charges; or
- optional additional services.

The charges applicable to a particular rental should be shown before booking confirmation or in the rental agreement.

## 6.8 Import Charges

Vehicle import costs may include:

### Vehicle Cost

The purchase price of the vehicle.

### Sourcing Fee

Where ShabaAutos sources a vehicle on the customer's behalf.

### Inspection Fee

Where an independent or ShabaAutos inspection is requested.

### Shipping and Logistics

Transportation from the vehicle location to the relevant shipping or destination point.

### Customs and Clearing

Applicable customs duties, levies, clearing and port-related charges.

### ShabaAutos Service Fee

The charge for coordinating the agreed import service.

These amounts should be itemised as clearly as reasonably possible in the customer's quotation.

## 6.9 Vehicle Verification Fees

Inspection or verification charges may depend on:

- vehicle location;
- inspection type;
- level of verification; and
- third-party provider charges.

## 6.10 Payment Processing

External payment providers may impose payment processing or currency conversion charges.

Where ShabaAutos directly imposes a payment-related fee, this should be disclosed before payment.

## 6.11 Taxes

Prices will indicate whether applicable taxes are included or charged separately where required.

## 6.12 Changes to Fees

ShabaAutos may change future pricing, membership plans or service charges.

A price change will not normally alter a transaction already confirmed at an agreed price unless:

- the customer agrees;
- a clearly disclosed variable third-party charge changes; or
- applicable law requires an adjustment.

Existing subscription members should receive reasonable information about material changes affecting future renewal.

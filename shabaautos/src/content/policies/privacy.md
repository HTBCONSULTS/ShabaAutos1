## 2.1 Our Commitment

ShabaAutos respects the privacy of customers, visitors, sellers, dealers and business partners.

This Privacy Policy explains how personal information may be collected, used, stored and shared when you interact with ShabaAutos.

## 2.2 Information We May Collect

Depending on the service used, we may collect:

### Identity Information

- name;
- date of birth where necessary;
- identification documents;
- photograph; and
- verification information.

### Contact Information

- telephone number;
- email address;
- residential or business address.

### Account Information

- username;
- account details;
- membership information;
- account activity.

### Transaction Information

- purchases;

- listings;
- rental bookings;
- import requests;
- payments;
- invoices;
- refunds.

### Vehicle Information

- registration details;
- VIN/chassis number;
- vehicle photographs;
- ownership information;
- documentation;
- inspection information.

### Technical Information

- IP address;
- browser information;
- device information;
- cookies; and
- website usage information.

### Communication Information

Messages or communications sent through:

- website forms;
- email;
- WhatsApp;
- telephone;
- customer support channels.

## 2.3 Why We Use Personal Information

Personal information may be used to:

- create and manage accounts;
- process transactions;
- provide vehicle listings;
- manage subscriptions;
- process rentals;
- provide import services;

- communicate with customers;
- prevent fraud;
- verify users and businesses;
- process payments;
- improve our services;
- maintain platform security;
- provide customer support;
- meet legal and regulatory obligations; and
- send marketing communications where permitted.

## 2.4 Legal Basis

Where required by applicable data protection law, ShabaAutos will process personal information on an appropriate lawful basis, which may include:

- consent;
- performance of a contract;
- compliance with legal obligations;
- legitimate business interests; or
- another basis permitted by law.

## 2.5 Sharing Information

We may share information where necessary with:

- payment providers;
- vehicle inspection providers;
- dealers and sellers;
- logistics providers;
- shipping companies;
- customs or clearing partners;
- insurers;
- technology providers;
- professional advisers;
- regulatory or law enforcement authorities where legally required.

We will not sell personal information as a commodity to unrelated third parties.

## 2.6 International Data Transfers

Some ShabaAutos services, particularly vehicle importation and technology services, may involve organisations located outside Nigeria.

Where personal information is transferred internationally, appropriate safeguards will be used where required by law.

## 2.7 Data Security

Reasonable technical and organisational measures will be used to protect personal information against:

- unauthorised access;
- loss;
- misuse;
- alteration; or
- inappropriate disclosure.

No digital system can guarantee absolute security.

## 2.8 Data Retention

Personal information will be retained only for as long as reasonably required for the purpose for which it was collected, legal requirements, dispute resolution, fraud prevention and legitimate business recordkeeping.

## 2.9 Your Rights

Subject to applicable law, individuals may have rights relating to:

- access to personal information;
- correction of inaccurate information;
- deletion in appropriate circumstances;
- restriction of processing;
- objection to certain processing;
- withdrawal of consent where processing relies on consent; and
- complaints about the handling of personal information.

## 2.10 Cookies

ShabaAutos may use cookies and similar technologies to operate the website, understand usage and improve the customer experience.

Where required, users will be provided with appropriate choices regarding non-essential cookies.

## 2.11 Marketing

Customers may opt out of direct marketing communications.

Service-related communications necessary for an active account or transaction may still be sent.

## 2.12 Privacy Contact

Privacy enquiries may be submitted through the ShabaAutos contact channels displayed on the website.

## 5.1 Rental Agreement

Every confirmed rental is subject to these general Rental Terms and any vehicle-specific rental agreement provided at booking or collection.

## 5.2 Driver Eligibility

Drivers must:

- meet the minimum age requirement stated for the vehicle;
- possess an appropriate valid driving licence;
- provide required identification;
- meet any applicable insurance requirements; and
- satisfy reasonable verification requirements.

## 5.3 Authorised Drivers

Only authorised drivers named or approved under the rental agreement may drive the vehicle.

## 5.4 Security Deposit

A refundable security deposit may be required.

The amount will be communicated before the rental is confirmed.

## 5.5 Vehicle Inspection

Customers should inspect the vehicle at collection and report existing damage.

Where practical, the vehicle condition may be recorded using:

- photographs;
- video;
- inspection sheet; or
- digital vehicle condition record.

## 5.6 Permitted Use

Rental vehicles must be used lawfully and responsibly.

Unless specifically authorised, customers must not:

- use the vehicle for racing;
- use the vehicle for unlawful activity;
- intentionally overload the vehicle;
- sub-rent the vehicle;
- drive outside permitted geographical areas;

- permit an unauthorised driver to operate it; or
- use it in a manner likely to cause unreasonable damage.

## 5.7 Fuel

The fuel arrangement applicable to the booking will be explained at collection or booking.

Charges may apply where the vehicle is returned below the agreed fuel level.

## 5.8 Damage

The customer must report accidents or material damage promptly.

Responsibility for repair costs will depend on:

- the circumstances;
- rental agreement;
- insurance arrangements;
- customer conduct; and
- applicable law.

## 5.9 Traffic Offences

The renter remains responsible for traffic fines, parking charges, toll charges and similar penalties resulting from use of the vehicle during the rental period, where legally applicable.

## 5.10 Late Return

Vehicles must be returned at the agreed time and location.

Late-return charges may apply where a vehicle is returned beyond an agreed grace period.

## 5.11 Breakdown

Customers should contact ShabaAutos or the designated assistance provider if a vehicle breaks down.

Customers should not commission major repairs without prior approval except where reasonably necessary in an emergency.

## 5.12 Accidents

In the event of an accident, the customer should:

1. prioritise safety;
2. contact emergency services where necessary;
3. notify ShabaAutos promptly;
4. obtain relevant information from involved parties;
5. avoid admitting liability without appropriate information; and
6. cooperate with any insurance or accident investigation.

## 5.13 Return Condition

Vehicles should be returned in substantially the same condition in which they were provided, allowing for reasonable use.

Charges may apply for:

- damage;
- missing items;
- excessive cleaning;
- unauthorised modifications; or
- other losses properly attributable to the renter.
